# Shark Tank feedback: what we changed

What reviewers said on the Collaborative Shark Tank feedback, and what we changed in response.

| Feedback | Teams | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Dark UI, hard to read | 5, 6, 8, 9, 11, 14 | Light theme is now the default. Dark mode is an opt-in toggle. |
| Text small and cramped | 9, 11, 12, 14 | Base text goes from 15px to 17px, with more padding, larger yes/no/unsure buttons, and a wider layout. |
| Flow and purpose unclear | 2, 7, 10, 11, 14 | Step bar (Fill out the form → Check before you sign → Sign and file) plus an intro that says the notes are optional. |
| Color coding unclear ("why is privacy yellow?") | 13 | A legend explains blue, yellow and green. |
| Notes like "approximate time is fine" should stay visible on the field | 12 | Each field has a hint that's always shown. |
| Add a way to attach photos or evidence | 5 | Optional photos or screenshots field for each incident. Prototype only: nothing is uploaded. |
| Test with a wider, more varied group than friends | 6, 9, 13, 14 | Testing plan, not code. Still to do. |

## Matched to the official Indiana form

Field wording and notes now follow the court's own Petition for an Order for Protection (PO-0100, rev. 07/2026) and Confidential Form (PO-0104), both from https://www.in.gov/courts/iocs/publications/po-forms/:

- The grounds list uses the exact wording from Question 1.
- Each incident asks for "who was there," as Question 8 does: list everyone present, including yourself.
- A new note for harassment-only petitions: PO-0100 says these can be granted only after notice to the other party and a hearing within 30 days.
- The privacy note quotes the form's warning that PO-0100 is public and may be sent to the respondent, and points to PO-0104.
